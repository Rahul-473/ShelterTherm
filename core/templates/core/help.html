Help & How ShelterTherm Works

Key Thermal Terms Explained
  • Thermal Conductivity (k, W/m·K): How easily heat moves through a material. Insulation (EPS, PUF, thatch) has very low k; metals (GI sheet) have very high k.
  • U-Value (W/m²·K): Heat passing through 1 m² of a wall or roof per degree temperature difference. Lower U-values keep heat out better.
  • Thermal Mass: Heavy materials (brick, concrete, adobe) store heat and delay temperature peaks.
  • Air Changes per Hour (ACH): How many times indoor air is replaced with outdoor air each hour. High night-time ACH (8–12) cools a shelter down overnight.
  • IMAC Comfort Band: The India Model for Adaptive Comfort estimates the acceptable indoor temperature range from the daily mean outdoor temperature.
Model Limitations

ShelterTherm is an early-stage design calculator. It simulates a single representative 24-hour design day assuming one well-mixed indoor air zone and 1D heat transfer through walls, roof, and floor. It does not model relative humidity, detailed CFD airflow, structural strength, construction costs, or full-year weather.
